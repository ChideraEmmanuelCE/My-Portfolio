03 / Approach

From blank page to shipped product.

01 / THINK

Find the real problem.

Clarify the user, the job to be done and the smallest useful version before adding complexity.

02 / BUILD

Make it feel obvious.

Design the flow, build the interface and connect the underlying systems without losing the human experience.

03 / SHIP

Turn the idea into something real.

Test, refine, deploy and keep improving. The finished product matters more than the demo.
