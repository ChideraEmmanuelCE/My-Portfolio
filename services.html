A few practical details.

Can you work with an agency remotely?

Yes. A defined feature or fix, an existing repository and clear review criteria provide a practical starting point. Availability and deadlines are confirmed for each project.

Is the starting price the final price?

The starting price covers the small scope listed above. Before work begins, the written quote confirms the total, included revisions, milestone payments and support. Extra features require a separate agreement.

No. The work improves the agreed website journey. Sales also depend on your offer, traffic, customer demand and follow-up.

Yes. Web or mobile application work is quoted after reviewing requirements, integrations and the delivery scope.
